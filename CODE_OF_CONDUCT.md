# Code of Conduct

Arkeyan Launcher is made by and for Skylanders fans. In its issues, pull requests, discussions and Discord:

- Be respectful and patient, with newcomers above all. Criticise code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks, and no sharing of someone's private information.
- No piracy: never share or ask for disc images, game files or download links for them.
- Stay on topic, and keep reports factual.

The owner moderates: a message or a contribution that breaks these rules can be edited or removed, and its author warned,
then blocked from the project. To report a problem, contact the owner privately on the
[Discord](https://discord.gg/UMQY4SAS3F).
