Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.3

## Additions
- The launcher in German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish and Finnish, with the Skylanders words as the games use them in each language. System default follows the system's language; Settings > Launcher > Language picks another.

## Fixes
- A figure's level and seat, the Collection's sort and its counts are translated, where they showed in English.
- Spyro's Adventure's and Giants' upgrade names, read from the disc, follow the launcher's language.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.8, Giants 0.8.8, Swap Force 0.5.7, Trap Team 0.1.1, SuperChargers 0.1.1, Imaginators 0.1.1.

## Known issues
- Achievements show in English in Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish and Finnish when the disc has no text in that language.
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)).
- SuperChargers and Imaginators: played to the Academy and the hub so far; not tested on Windows or Linux yet.
- Not tested on a real Linux PC yet.
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.

## Updating
From 0.6 or later: the launcher updates itself.
