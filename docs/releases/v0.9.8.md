Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.8

## Additions
- **Beta updates**: a new option in Settings > Updates, Receive betas, offers each test build of the next version and then the final one. It is off by default, and on in a launcher installed from a test build.
- A test build shows a Beta badge next to its version, in the sidebar and in Settings > Updates, and writes its version at the top of its log.
- Turning Receive betas off on a test build offers the latest final version. Before going back to an older version, the launcher copies your saves and figures to a dated folder next to them, and warns that those made with the test build may not open in it.

## Fixes
- Giants: in a maximized window wider than 16:9, the menus no longer jump between a full-width picture and a narrower one with black sides.
- Trap Team: in an ultrawide window, the darkening behind the main menu and the cutscenes now reaches the corners of the window instead of stopping at the middle 16:9.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.14, Giants 0.8.14, Swap Force 0.5.13, Trap Team 0.1.7, SuperChargers 0.1.7, Imaginators 0.1.7.

## Known issues
- Swap Force: the core in the tutorial's volcano is drawn with dark streaks.
- Spyro's Adventure on the Steam Deck plays at 30 FPS in levels.
- Xbox 360 and Xbox One portals on Windows: not confirmed on a real PC yet.
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)).
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.

## Thanks
To everyone who reported a bug, sent a log or tested a build for this version: GobGub (who recorded the Giants menu and sent the logs), Suolumi and the testers on the Discord.

From 0.6 or later: the launcher updates itself.
