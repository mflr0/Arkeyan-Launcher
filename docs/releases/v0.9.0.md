Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.0

## Additions
- A new in-game menu in the launcher's look: F3 opens a panel beside the game (volume, Toy Box, achievements, settings, performance, report a problem), and the Toy Box, Achievements, Settings and Controls pages sit beside a rail, in the running game's colour; messages appear in the corners.
- Skylanders: SuperChargers and Skylanders: Imaginators, built from your own disc like the other games (SuperChargers 0.1.0, Imaginators 0.1.0).
- Player profiles: a nickname, a picture and a colour each, with their own Toy Box, saves, keys and settings; switch from the top of the sidebar. The games show the profile's name and picture, and Player 2 can have a profile of their own.
- A new debug screen (Shift+F3): frame, CPU and GPU times, latency, memory, the machine and a frame-time graph, in a compact or a full view; F3+R records ten seconds of performance to a file.
- "Press Start" shows the button of the device in use in SuperChargers and Imaginators, as in Swap Force.
- Nightmare from the start in Swap Force and Imaginators settings, where the game locks it.
- Creation Crystals tinted by their element in the Collection and the Toy Box.

## Changes
- A new logo.
- Imaginators' logo and background are read from your disc.
- Each game's PLAY button, focus ring and sidebar mark in its own colour.
- The sidebar's games scroll on their own, Collection and Settings stay at the bottom; "Coming later" lists SuperChargers Racing, Cloud Patrol and Lost Islands.
- The game page keeps its place when the game starts.
- The portal panel shows every place of the game (Trap Team's trap, SuperChargers' vehicles).
- Credits: designed by dorydev, made by mflr0, UI/UX by Gumelia.

## Fixes
- Imaginators and SuperChargers: lighting no longer goes dark in some levels (the deferred passes).
- A Creation Crystal made in the launcher is accepted by Imaginators.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.8, Giants 0.8.8, Swap Force 0.5.7, Trap Team 0.1.1, SuperChargers 0.1.0, Imaginators 0.1.0.

## Known issues
- HD textures: the kit can build them, but the launcher does not offer them yet (planned for 0.9.2).
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive of its machine-learning guess on an unsigned program that downloads a compiler and builds a new executable ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)). Restore the file from Protection history and add an exclusion for `%APPDATA%\Skylanders\kit`.
- SuperChargers and Imaginators: played to the Academy and the hub so far; not tested on Windows or Linux yet.
- Not tested on a real Linux PC yet.
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.

## Updating
From 0.6 or later: the launcher updates itself.
