Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.6

## Additions
- **Discord Rich Presence**: Discord shows the game, the level you are in (in the game's language), the Skylander on the portal, the play time and two-player games, with buttons to the project. Settings > Launcher > Discord turns it off.
- **Steam Deck Game Mode**: the games show their picture under Game Mode as well as in Desktop Mode.
- **Xbox portals on Windows**: the Xbox One portal through Windows' own Xbox driver, the Xbox 360 portal through WinUSB (one step in Device Manager, described in the README). Still to be confirmed on a real PC.
- The game kits are built by GitHub Actions from the public sources, for every release.
- Ultrawide: the scene follows the window's shape live, when it is resized or goes fullscreen.
- Either stick aims the quick swap wheel.
- Frame pacing: the drop to 30 FPS only happens when 60 is lost for 5 seconds, and is off by default on dedicated graphics cards; VSync no longer queues frames on screens whose refresh is not a multiple of 60 (144 Hz).
- Windows: if Direct3D 12 shows no picture, the game switches to Vulkan by itself.
- Bug reports: a log file can be dropped into an issue form.
- Install on macOS: clear steps when Xcode's Metal shader compiler is missing.

## Fixes
- Giants, Direct3D 12: black, yellow and purple patterns on far scenery (one of the game's blend settings made Direct3D 12 refuse a pipeline, so those objects were drawn with another object's settings).
- Giants: the tutorial's waterfall disappeared, or turned speckled, each time a boomerang flew by (a shader instruction read a value after it was overwritten; the same rule now holds for every game's shaders).
- Swap Force at 60 FPS: characters turn their heads at the normal speed in dialogues.
- Swap Force: a movie read that fails no longer ends in a green screen; disc reads are retried.
- Spyro's Adventure: no more black flickers at the start, on the hub and in levels; no more grass-coloured squares along grass edges.
- Imaginators: the created character's portrait appears.
- Trap Team: the voices follow their own language setting, and LB can be bound (Attack 3).
- Linux and the Steam Deck: the Xbox 360 portal answers (the game read 64 bytes where the portal sends 32, and the gamepad driver took the portal; it no longer shows as a second gamepad).
- Collection: no crash when choosing a game with fewer portal seats.
- Add a figure: the full "needs SuperChargers" label, like every other game.
- Sharper icons on Retina and scaled Windows screens.
- Test the portal: the copied result is readable on Windows.
- Toy Box: a swap from the wheel or the Toy Box window replaces the figure in place.
- Windows: installing a game no longer stops with "Access is denied" when an antivirus holds a file for a moment.
- FSR 3: less trail behind fast-moving characters.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.12, Giants 0.8.12, Swap Force 0.5.11, Trap Team 0.1.5, SuperChargers 0.1.5, Imaginators 0.1.5.

## Known issues
- Giants: in a maximized window wider than 16:9 the menus can jitter; set Widescreen to 16:9 in the game's settings.
- Swap Force: the core in the tutorial's volcano is drawn with dark streaks.
- Xbox 360 and Xbox One portals on Windows: not confirmed on a real PC yet.
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)).
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.
