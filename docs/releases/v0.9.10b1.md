Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.10b1 (beta)

A test build of 0.9.10. Report anything that goes wrong with the game log.

## Additions
- **Portal marks**: each game in the sidebar now shows its own portal, which lights up when you point at it or pick it.
- **Figure cards**: every figure card looks the same everywhere, with the figure's element and type icons. Sensei and Swap Force figures get the icons they have in the game.
- **Traps**: a trap with a villain inside shows that villain's picture.
- **Add a figure**: each version of a figure now says which game it comes from.

## Fixes
- SuperChargers: Hammer Slam Bowser and Turbo Charge Donkey Kong are Wii-only, so the collection no longer offers them.
- Linux: a crash report now says where in the game the crash happened, which helps us fix it.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.17, Giants 0.8.17, Swap Force 0.5.16, Trap Team 0.1.11, SuperChargers 0.1.10, Imaginators 0.1.10.

## Known issues
- Swap Force: the picture can still turn plain green after a figure swap in Wildwoods. We're looking into it.
- Spyro's Adventure on the Steam Deck plays at 30 FPS in levels.
- Xbox 360 and Xbox One portals on Windows: not confirmed on a real PC yet.
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)).
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.

## Thanks
To everyone who tests this build and sends a log.
