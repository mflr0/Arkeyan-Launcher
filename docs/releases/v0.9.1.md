Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.1

## Additions
- On Windows, a notice before the first install or update: why Windows Defender may report the builder, the launcher or a game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`, and how to let them run, step by step. It is shown once. The launcher and its source code have been submitted to Microsoft as a false positive; the review is pending.

## Fixes
- Imaginators' logo: the letters are no longer broken; the logo keeps the light it sits in on the title screen.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.8, Giants 0.8.8, Swap Force 0.5.7, Trap Team 0.1.1, SuperChargers 0.1.0, Imaginators 0.1.0.

## Known issues
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)).
- SuperChargers and Imaginators: the story movies play black; the Keyboard and Controller tabs show Trap Team's actions.
- SuperChargers and Imaginators: played to the Academy and the hub so far; not tested on Windows or Linux yet.
- Not tested on a real Linux PC yet.
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.

## Updating
From 0.6 or later: the launcher updates itself.
