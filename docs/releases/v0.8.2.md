Arkeyan Launcher 0.8.2

## Additions
- SuperChargers, experimental and off by default (Settings > Launcher): installed from your own disc like the other games; the intro, the rift tunnel with a vehicle and the first area on foot play.
- A file check at every start: the kit and every installed game are compared with their file lists; a missing or changed file (an antivirus quarantine, for instance) is named, with how to restore it and a Repair button.

## Changes
- Trap Team is no longer experimental: it is always in your games (Trap Team 0.1.0).
- Smoother play at 60 FPS: the automatic drop to 30 only on a lasting slowdown, never during a load, and a quick return to 60.
- Faster frames in every game (vertex data checked once per frame; Spyro's Adventure's depth copy four times cheaper; Trap Team no longer spins while it waits).
- "Resolution scale: Auto" steps down by itself at the next start when the graphics card limits the frame rate (a scale you chose is never changed).
- One icon per meaning on every button; removals in red with a bin.
- Trap Team's page, focus ring and Home card in its title's red.
- The loading spinner turns about its own centre.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.7, Giants 0.8.7, Swap Force 0.5.6, Trap Team 0.1.0, SuperChargers 0.0.1.

## Known issues
- SuperChargers is experimental: tested as far as the first area on foot, 30 FPS, Xbox button prompts, generic figure and vehicle data.
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: this is a false positive. Restore the file from Protection history and add an exclusion for `%APPDATA%\Skylanders\kit`.
- Not tested on a real Linux PC yet. Portable copies not tried on Windows or Linux yet.
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Trap Team: a real Traptanium portal for Wii and PS3 is reported as incompatible by the game, and the Xbox One Traptanium portal is not found; the emulated portal works.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.

## Updating
From 0.6 or later: the launcher updates itself.
