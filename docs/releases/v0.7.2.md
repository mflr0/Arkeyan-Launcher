Arkeyan Launcher 0.7.2 cleans up the install screen.

## What's new
- **A clearer install screen.** Nothing is cut off any more, in any language or window size, and the card fits its content. The buttons and the portal panel stay in place while a game installs.
- **Interrupted installs.** The screen says plainly how to carry on, and a game you already have still plays without the update.
- **The portal panel** has a proper Toy Box button.

## The games
No change: the games stay at the same versions, nothing to rebuild.

## Known limits
- This release has not been tested on a real Windows or Linux PC yet.
- Trap Team is experimental, off by default (Settings > Launcher), and tested as far as the hub: expect bugs and crashes beyond it. Above 30 FPS, its liquids ripple too fast. Capturing a villain in a trap has not been verified, its voices follow the text language, and LB is not offered as a key yet.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.

## Updating
From Arkeyan Launcher 0.6 or 0.7: it updates itself.
