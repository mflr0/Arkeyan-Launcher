Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.10b2 (beta)

A test build of 0.9.10. Report anything that goes wrong with the game log.

## Additions
- **Settings**: game settings now sit in four tabs (Picture, Controls, Sound and language, Manage), and the launcher's own settings in four more (General, Controls, Portal, Help). A dot marks what a game changes from the common controls.
- **Home**: picks up your last game, or helps you get started if you're new. Drop a disc there and it selects its game.
- **Game colors**: each game has its own color on its badge and pages.
- **Collection**: figures are sorted into titled sections that follow the sort order. Figures the chosen game can't use are hidden (Works in > All games shows them again).
- **Starter packs**: each game offers its own starter pack.
- **Add a figure**: the + on an empty seat opens Add a figure, already showing only what that seat takes, and no longer says "needs <game>" once that game is installed.
- **Controller shortcuts**: add and clear figures from the pad, in the collection and in the Toy Box.
- **Toy Box**: the mouse wheel scrolls the list, and the Mouse row comes first in Controls.
- **Report a problem**: one button saves the report, then opens GitHub. The report holds only the chosen game's log.

## Fixes
- Swap Force: the picture no longer turns plain green after a figure swap in Wildwoods. Tell us if you still see it.
- Toy Box: the overlay is drawn at your screen's resolution, so it's sharp on large screens.
- Imaginators and the Toy Box: seat labels go on two rows instead of being cut to "...".
- Privacy: reports and game logs no longer hold your account name, computer name, or the names of external drives and network shares. Logs already on disk get cleaned the next time the launcher starts.
- Home: a file that isn't a usable disc no longer leaves the game stuck on a failed disc.
- Picking "Custom" window size for one game no longer shows "Custom" for the others.
- The update banner's error message no longer gets cut off.
- Sidebar: the games show their initials again instead of portal marks.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.10.0, Giants 0.8.18, Swap Force 0.5.17, Trap Team 0.1.12, SuperChargers 0.1.11, Imaginators 0.1.11.

## Known issues
- Spyro's Adventure on the Steam Deck plays at 30 FPS in levels.
- Xbox 360 and Xbox One portals on Windows: not confirmed on a real PC yet.
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)).
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.
