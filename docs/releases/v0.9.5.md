Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.5

## Additions
- Windows: each game's settings choose the graphics API, **Direct3D 12** (the default) or **Vulkan** (Video > Display); F3 shows the one in use. If the chosen one can't start, the game uses the other.
- **Find my edition**: for a disc edition that isn't supported yet, the launcher reads the disc and copies a short report for a [New disc edition](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher/issues/new?template=new_disc_edition.yml) issue: IDs, region, languages, fingerprints. Nothing from the game itself.
- **Copy disc details** beside the "not supported yet" message.
- Another edition with the same game code as a supported one (only the disc's header differs) is accepted.
- The figure catalogue reads SuperChargers' and Imaginators' own toy tables: Trap Team's Dark Wildfire, Dark Snap Shot and Dark Food Fight, its Legendary and Nitro figures, the "Instant" editions, SuperChargers' 13 racing villains and their 15 vehicles, and more. 311 toys and 420 variants.
- The Xbox One portal (`1430:09AA`) on macOS and Linux. On Windows the portal test names it; Xbox One portals on Windows come in the next version.
- Xbox portals are recognized by the kind of device they are, not only by a list of models; an unknown one is named in the game's log.
- A **Discord** button beside the GitHub one.

## Fixes
- NVIDIA graphics cards: Trap Team's title logo has its white glow again, and reflections light the 3D models as on other cards (cube maps were bound through the wrong kind of view). Screen-space passes no longer shift the picture by a pixel, and a draw's unwritten pictures are kept, as on the console.
- Direct3D 12: each of a draw's pictures gets its own blending and channels.
- Linux, fullscreen: the picture no longer stays black while the sound plays (seen with an NVIDIA driver on Wayland).
- Windows and macOS: the game and its sound keep their speed when the window is behind another one.
- The game's sound and the launcher's music never play on a portal's speaker; they stay on the default output.
- Test the portal: Wii, PlayStation and PC portals that worked were missing from the result in 0.9.4 ("No portal found").
- Test the portal: a portal that opens but doesn't answer shows "Found, not answering" and why; **Copy** gives every portal's full result for an issue.
- Test the portal, Xbox 360 portals: "Real portal" only when the portal answers; the game's log shows what it sent.
- Windows: Test the portal and the game's log list each device that may be a portal, with its driver, and how to give a portal moved to another driver back to Windows.
- Linux: the original Wii portal and the black-cord Swap Force portal can be written to.
- Giants with a real portal: no more "Please put a Skylander on the Portal of Power" loop with a portal that numbers its figures anew after a reset.
- The portal chip on the game's page sees Xbox 360 and Xbox One portals.
- Portals are named by model, not console.
- Linux: `portable.txt` and a kit beside the AppImage are found.
- Logs: the game's log starts with the date and time of the run; Settings > Support shows which run **Copy game log** carries; each run is copied once.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.11, Giants 0.8.11, Swap Force 0.5.10, Trap Team 0.1.4, SuperChargers 0.1.4, Imaginators 0.1.4.

## Known issues
- FSR 3: a fast-moving character leaves a slight trail.
- Spyro's Adventure on Linux: yellow-green blocks on the grass at a 2560x1440 window.
- Achievements show in English in Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish and Finnish when the disc has no text in that language.
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)).
- Steam Deck: in Game Mode the game's picture may not appear; Desktop Mode works.
- SuperChargers and Imaginators: not tested on Windows or Linux yet.
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.

## Updating
From 0.6 or later: the launcher updates itself.
