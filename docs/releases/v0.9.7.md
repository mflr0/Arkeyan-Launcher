Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.7

## Additions
- **Discord Rich Presence**: the Skylander on a real portal now shows too, with its picture, its name in the running game and its level.
- **SuperChargers** takes traps, and **Imaginators** takes vehicles and traps, as on the console: the figures and the Toy Box can put them on the portal.
- Imaginators and SuperChargers: the button prompts on chests and the "press repeatedly" prompts follow the controller in use, like the other games.
- The "game stopped unexpectedly" dialog is now the launcher's own, with Report, Copy log and Close.

## Fixes
- Trap Team: a Wii Traptanium portal no longer stops answering until the game restarts (the portal's sounds could hold back its commands).
- Swapping figures quickly (the swap wheel, the Toy Box or the launcher's window): the old figure leaves before the new one arrives, as with a real portal, and a figure chosen while the game is still bringing the previous one in waits its turn instead of being lost.
- macOS: an even 60 FPS on 120 Hz displays (ProMotion MacBooks), each frame held for two refreshes.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.13, Giants 0.8.13, Swap Force 0.5.12, Trap Team 0.1.6, SuperChargers 0.1.6, Imaginators 0.1.6.

## Known issues
- Giants: in a maximized window wider than 16:9 the menus can jitter; set Widescreen to 16:9 in the game's settings.
- Swap Force: the core in the tutorial's volcano is drawn with dark streaks.
- Trap Team: in an ultrawide window, the darkening behind the main menu and the cutscenes covers only the middle 16:9.
- Spyro's Adventure on the Steam Deck plays at 30 FPS in levels.
- Xbox 360 and Xbox One portals on Windows: not confirmed on a real PC yet.
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)).
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.
