Arkeyan Launcher 0.8.1

## Changes
- "Uninstall everything..." in Settings > Launcher is red, as the button that confirms it.
- Messages stay longer: at least 5 seconds in the launcher and over the games.
- The Trap Team switch says what it does, and a message confirms Trap Team is added to or hidden from your games.
- "Report a problem" says the report is anonymous and how to share it (added to an issue on GitHub).
- Credits: designed by dorydev, made by mflr0.

## Fixes
- Fixed a shader cache made by an older install being reused after an update that changed the compile options, which could leave shaders without their Windows version.
- Fixed two kinds of texture reads in vertex shaders that could not be compiled.
- Fixed a family of vector instructions being read as unknown in the game code (none occurs in the four games today).

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.6, Giants 0.8.6, Swap Force 0.5.5, Trap Team 0.0.5. Updating a game compiles its shaders again once.

## Known issues
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: this is a false positive. Restore the file from Protection history and add an exclusion for `%APPDATA%\Skylanders\kit`.
- Not tested on a real Linux PC yet. Portable copies not tried on Windows or Linux yet.
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Trap Team: a real Traptanium portal for Wii and PS3 is reported as incompatible by the game, and the Xbox One Traptanium portal is not found; the emulated portal works. The RT in some hints' text and the LT icon of the villain gauge still show Xbox buttons.
- Trap Team is experimental and off by default (Settings > Launcher), tested as far as the hub. Spy Rise looks almost black, voices follow the text language, LB is not offered as a key.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.

## Updating
From 0.6 or later: the launcher updates itself.
