Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.4

## Additions
- AMD FSR 3 upscaling in all six games: the 3D scene is drawn smaller and upscaled to the window, the HUD and menus stay sharp at full size. FSR 3 in the game's settings, with five quality presets; F3 in game changes both live. Off by default.
- FSR 3 Native AA: a fifth FSR 3 quality, at full resolution (FSR 3 as anti-aliasing).
- Each game's settings in a clearer order: Video (Display, Image quality, Textures), Keyboard, Controller, Sound, Language, Disc, Saves, Game. Every setting is kept.
- HD textures for Spyro's Adventure (2.3 GB) and Giants (4.8 GB) too: "Use HD textures" in the game's settings, for all six games. It works on its own now: before, turning off the custom texture packs turned the HD textures off too.
- Frame generation, custom texture packs and texture dumps: greyed in the game's settings, coming later.
- The frame rate limit is 30 or 60: a higher one chosen before is now 60.
- The Toy Box no longer shows a figure as out of health when an adventure or a level starts: only when the game asks for it again after it played in the level.
- 5.1 surround sound in every game: Speakers in the game's settings (Auto, Stereo, 5.1). Auto follows the output device.
- The Toy Box in its own window: while a game runs, the launcher opens its Toy Box beside it (a second monitor works) to put, swap and remove figures; the game shows the change at once.
- A real portal and the virtual Toy Box together: one player on a real figure, the other on a virtual one, in every game with two players.

## Fixes
- SuperChargers: races no longer crash as they start.
- macOS: Wii, Wii U, PlayStation and PC portals work again (some could not be written to).
- macOS: Xbox 360 and Xbox One portals work with nothing to install (libusb is no longer needed).
- Linux: Spyro's Adventure installs (a path with an apostrophe broke the build).
- Linux: the README gives the udev rules for Xbox portals too.
- Test the portal: it reports a portal the game can't talk to as a problem, not a success.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.10, Giants 0.8.10, Swap Force 0.5.9, Trap Team 0.1.3, SuperChargers 0.1.3, Imaginators 0.1.3.

## Known issues
- FSR 3: a fast-moving character leaves a slight trail.
- Spyro's Adventure on Linux: yellow-green blocks on the grass at a 2560x1440 window.
- Achievements show in English in Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish and Finnish when the disc has no text in that language.
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)).
- SuperChargers and Imaginators: not tested on Windows or Linux yet.
- Not tested on a real Linux PC yet.
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.

## Updating
From 0.6 or later: the launcher updates itself.
