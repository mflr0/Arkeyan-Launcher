Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.9

## Additions
- **Games folder**: pick where the games get built (Settings). HD textures and game data move with them.
- **Controls for every game**: Settings > Controls sets keys, controller buttons, mouse and rumble once for all games. A game's own Keyboard and Controller tabs can still change a row for that game only, and an arrow puts the global value back. Your current keys are kept.
- **Pad buttons for the wheel, Toy Box and menu**: pick which controller buttons open them, in Settings > Controls or in the F3 menu.
- **Keys**: a Toy Box key that another action or a game also uses shows up in red in Settings and in the F3 menu.
- **Window size**: the game opens at the size you last left it. Settings > Video > Window size also lets you pick a fixed size.
- **Widescreen Fill**: also covers a 16:10 screen (like the Steam Deck) with no black bars.
- **Drag and drop**: drag a figure onto a seat in the in-game Toy Box too. In the Collection, the seats a figure can go to light up while you drag it, and you can drag a figure from one seat to another.
- **Figure pictures**: a figure shows the picture from its own game by default. Pick another game's picture, or your own, in Settings > Launcher or on the figure's page.
- **Imaginators**: add Imaginite chests from the Play page. They show up at the Academy.
- **SuperChargers and Imaginators**: a Trap seat in the Collection and on the "On the portal" card.
- **SuperChargers**: the Europe (French, Dutch) disc works.
- **Trap Team: Nightmare from the start**: an option in the game's settings (Game tab) unlocks Nightmare without beating the story first. Your save isn't changed.
- **Music volume** for the launcher's game pages.
- **Portable copy**: it now takes your game settings along, and the HD textures too if you turn on "Include HD textures".
- **Disc check**: once a disc is verified, the launcher compares every file with a good copy of the same edition, so a damaged disc image gets caught before it stops a game at a loading screen.
- **Linux window controls**: on Wayland, the launcher window has a title bar with the usual buttons.
- **Toy Box**: double click a figure on the portal to take it off. While a figure's versions are open, the other cards don't light up under the mouse anymore, and clicking next to them closes the versions.
- **Faster launcher**: pictures and the collection load in the background, so the launcher doesn't freeze while they load.

## Fixes
- HD textures: the pack's extra detail now actually shows in game.
- Swap Force: the core in the tutorial's volcano no longer has dark streaks.
- Swap Force: swapping halves with the figure wheel no longer opens the Toy Box.
- Swap Force: the swap wheel no longer gets stuck on the bottom choice, and the keyboard moves it again.
- Spyro's Adventure: the pause screen fills a wide window.
- Trap Team: in an ultrawide window, the flash into the trap screen fills the whole window.
- Trap Team: a Traptanium portal plays the villains' sounds.
- Imaginators: fewer stutters the first time you enter a zone.
- Windows: a Traptanium portal no longer freezes the game.
- Steam Deck: the in-game menu and the Toy Box don't bring up Steam's on-screen keyboard anymore.
- Discord: with a real portal, the status shows the figure the game is using, and the Arkeyan Launcher icon shows up again.
- Toy Box, figure wheel and F3 menu: one key press moves one step, and the game's own keys (like WASD) don't move them anymore.
- All games: getting each frame's draws ready costs less processor time.
- Every game: a game built with a beta is built again once, so it gets all of these fixes.

## Known issues
- Swap Force: the picture can still turn plain green after a figure swap in Wildwoods. We're looking into it.
- Spyro's Adventure on the Steam Deck plays at 30 FPS in levels.
- Xbox 360 and Xbox One portals on Windows: not confirmed on a real PC yet.
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)).
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.
