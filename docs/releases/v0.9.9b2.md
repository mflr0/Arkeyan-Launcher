Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.9b2 (beta)

A test build of 0.9.9. Report anything that goes wrong with the game log.

## Additions
- **Games folder**: choose where the games are built (Settings). The HD textures and the game data move with them.
- **Disc check**: once a disc is verified, the launcher compares every file with a good copy of the same edition, so a damaged disc image is caught before it can stop a game at a loading screen.
- **Widescreen Fill**: also covers a 16:10 screen without black bars.
- **SuperChargers and Imaginators**: a Trap seat in the Collection and on the "On the portal" card.
- **Keys**: a Toy Box key that another action or a game also uses is shown in red in Settings, and in the F3 menu.
- **Faster launcher**: pictures and the collection load in the background, so the launcher no longer stops while they load.
- **Trap Team: Nightmare from the start**: an option in the game's settings (Game tab) opens Nightmare difficulty without beating the story first. Your save is not changed.
- **SuperChargers**: the Europe (French, Dutch) disc is supported.

## Fixes
- HD textures: the pack's extra detail now shows clearly in game.
- Imaginators: fewer stutters the first time a zone is entered.
- Swap Force: the picture no longer turns green after some in-level events.
- Swap Force: the core in the tutorial's volcano is drawn without dark streaks.
- Swap Force: swapping halves with the figure wheel no longer opens the Toy Box.
- Windows: a Traptanium portal no longer freezes the game.
- Steam Deck: the in-game menu and the Toy Box no longer bring up Steam's on-screen keyboard.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.15, Giants 0.8.15, Swap Force 0.5.14, Trap Team 0.1.8, SuperChargers 0.1.8, Imaginators 0.1.8.

## Known issues
- Spyro's Adventure on the Steam Deck plays at 30 FPS in levels.
- Xbox 360 and Xbox One portals on Windows: not confirmed on a real PC yet.
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)).
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.
