Arkeyan Launcher 0.8.0

## Additions
- Mouse support in all four games, off by default; a switch per game on its keys page and in the F3 Commands page.
  - Left click: attack 1. Right click: attack 2.
  - Every mouse button and the wheel can be bound to any action.
  - The pointer is held in the window during play and released in the F3 menu, the Toy Box, on Alt-Tab and on focus loss; a click in the window takes it back and is not sent to the game.
- Mouse clicks in the F3 menu and the Toy Box.
- Portable copy: an installed game exported as a single `.app`, `.exe` or `.AppImage`, playable without the launcher or the disc image.
  - Shares the installed game's saves and settings; the two never run at the same time.
  - Carries the figures and keys at the time of the export; never updates.
  - For personal use only.
- Debug screen in F3: game and version, frame rate, memory, graphics card, level.
- Each game's main theme on its page, read from the disc, with a fade between pages and a mute button.
- Automatic drop to 30 FPS as a switch per game, on its settings page and in F3. On by default.
- Each game's version under its name in the sidebar.
- One launcher at a time per data folder: a second start brings the open one to the front; a game shortcut asks the open one to play.
- Controller button hints on the settings pages and in every dialog.

## Changes
- New in-game menu.
- Toy Box navigation in rows (seats, search, figures): up and down keep the column, each row remembers its position, one focus ring on every screen.
- Toy Box keys (F6 or Tab, F7, F8) can be rebound like the other keys.
- Games installed as `Skylanders <game>`, each with its own icon.
- Figures show their newest picture; a swapper's half with no picture of its own shows its other half's.
- The game installer is now called Arkeyan-Builder.
- Faster installs: about 2.5 minutes for Trap Team and Giants on an Apple Silicon Mac, down from 9 to 11; Swap Force and Spyro's Adventure faster too.
- The install's time left follows each game's own steps.
- Lower memory use for pictures and lower processor use when idle.

## Fixes
- Fixed some of Giants' shaders missing after an install on Windows, which left the picture black. Existing installs are repaired by the update.
- Fixed thin black outlines around some surfaces at a resolution scale above 1 (Swap Force, Trap Team).
- Fixed a few reflections translated incorrectly.
- Fixed Trap Team no longer reading figures after a trap's first voice line, which blocked swaps with a trap on the portal and later removed the Skylander.
- Fixed Trap Team's water and lava ripples running too fast above 30 FPS.
- Fixed capturing a villain in Trap Team: the trap keeps it across a restart (tested with Sheep Creep in the tutorial).
- Fixed uninstalling, clearing caches or deleting data touching saves kept in a custom folder or left by a migration.
- Fixed a running game being removable.
- Fixed launcher files left half-written after an interrupted write.
- Fixed several Collection issues: seats, backups following a renamed or deleted figure, restoring another figure's backup, export overwriting a file, imports of huge or duplicate files, figure names and labels out of the game's limits, out-of-range resolution scale in launcher.ini, comments of launcher.ini lost on save.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.5, Giants 0.8.5, Swap Force 0.5.4, Trap Team 0.0.4.

## Known issues
- Not tested on a real Windows or Linux PC yet. Portable copies not tried on Windows or Linux yet.
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus (planned for 0.8.1).
- Trap Team is experimental and off by default (Settings > Launcher), tested as far as the hub. Spy Rise looks almost black, voices follow the text language, LB is not offered as a key.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.

## Updating
From 0.6 or 0.7: the launcher updates itself.
