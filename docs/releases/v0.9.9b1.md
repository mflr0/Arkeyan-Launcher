Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.9b1 (beta)

A test build of 0.9.9. Report anything that goes wrong with the game log.

## Additions
- **Linux window controls**: on Wayland, the launcher's window now has a title bar with the usual buttons to move, minimize, maximize and close it.
- **Toy Box**: double click a figure on the portal to take it off. While a figure's versions are open, the other cards no longer light up under the mouse, and a click beside them closes the versions.

## Fixes
- Discord: with a real portal, the status now shows the figure the game plays with.
- Toy Box, figure wheel and F3 menu: a key press moves the focus by one step, and the game's own keys (such as WASD) no longer move it.
- All games: preparing each frame's draws takes less time on the computer's processor.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.14, Giants 0.8.14, Swap Force 0.5.13, Trap Team 0.1.7, SuperChargers 0.1.7, Imaginators 0.1.7.

## Known issues
- Swap Force: the core in the tutorial's volcano is drawn with dark streaks.
- Spyro's Adventure on the Steam Deck plays at 30 FPS in levels.
- Xbox 360 and Xbox One portals on Windows: not confirmed on a real PC yet.
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)).
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.

## Thanks
To everyone who tests this build and sends a log.
