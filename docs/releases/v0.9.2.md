Arkeyan Launcher 0.9.2

## Additions
- HD textures: a switch in each game's settings (off by default) builds the game's textures at twice their size from the disc, in the background; turning it off removes them. It takes 2 to 3 GB of disk per game.
- SuperChargers and Imaginators have their own Keyboard and Controller lists, with their own actions (SuperChargers' vehicle controls); Trap Team's list is no longer shown for them.

## Changes
- A game with no language chosen starts in the launcher's language when its disc has it, and its Language tab shows that language.
- The Windows game kit is smaller (about 45 MB instead of 67) and built the same way every time from the same source; its builder downloads and unpacks the compiler itself.

## Fixes
- SuperChargers and Imaginators: the story movies are no longer black (the install now waits for the movies' shaders). Both games move to 0.1.1, offered as an update.
- SuperChargers on macOS: the game's icon shows in the Dock again.
- Trap Team: with the keyboard or another controller style, the RT sign above a cannon or a chest shows the button alone, without the 360's "RT" over it.
- A damaged disc image whose folders loop no longer makes the launcher run out of memory at every start: it is refused.
- Collection > Manage: the focus follows the opened tab, and the Restore buttons of a figure's copies are no longer cut.
- Collection: choosing a figure for a seat starts at the top of the shelf; a card's tip no longer stays over the cards while the controller moves the focus.
- The portable copy can no longer be opened for a game that is not installed.

## Games
Spyro's Adventure 0.9.8, Giants 0.8.8, Swap Force 0.5.7, Trap Team 0.1.1, SuperChargers 0.1.1, Imaginators 0.1.1.

## Known issues
- Windows Defender may report Arkeyan-Builder.exe, the launcher or a built game as `Trojan:Win32/Bearfoos.A!ml`: a false positive ([why, and what to do](https://github.com/mflr0/Arkeyan-Launcher#windows-defender-alerts)).
- SuperChargers and Imaginators: played to the Academy and the hub so far; not tested on Windows or Linux yet.
- Not tested on a real Linux PC yet.
- The mouse does nothing in the games' own menus.
- Apple Silicon Mac required (M1 or later); the first launch on macOS 15 and later goes through Open Anyway.

## Updating
From 0.6 or later: the launcher updates itself.
